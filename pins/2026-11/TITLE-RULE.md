# Pinterest title rule (from 2026-11-03)
Keyword first, hook second. Pattern: "<search phrase buyers type>: <hook from the pin image>". Under 100 characters.
Search phrase = the product category in the buyer's words (notary journal, loan signing agent checklist, household budget and bill tracker, estate planning binder, SOP template for small business, support broker planner). Never lead with the hook or the brand.
Lesson pins: "<Audience> Tips: <lesson line>".
Current titles for scheduled pins: pinterest-titles-seo.csv. Weekly top-up task must follow this rule for every new Pinterest post.
